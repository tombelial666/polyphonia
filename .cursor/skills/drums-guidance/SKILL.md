# Drums Guidance

## Goal

Adapt musical advice for drums and percussion so rhythmic suggestions are felt as groove structure, momentum, and arrangement support.

## Use With

- `composer`
- `music-theory-teacher`
- `producer`
- `solfege-teacher`
- `music-director`

## Workflow

1. Identify meter, tempo range, and groove family when known.
2. Separate core pulse from fills, accents, and transition devices.
3. Keep kick, snare, cymbal, and ghost-note roles clear.
4. Recommend density changes by section rather than constant maximal activity.
5. If teaching, describe subdivision and accent placement explicitly.

## Guardrails

- do not write impossible limb combinations as if they are trivial
- do not confuse groove identity with random busy fills
- keep arrangement advice tied to section energy and momentum
