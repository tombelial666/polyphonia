# Bass Guidance

## Goal

Adapt musical advice for bass so it supports harmony, time feel, and low-end clarity rather than duplicating guitar or piano thinking.

## Use With

- `composer`
- `music-theory-teacher`
- `producer`
- `solfege-teacher`
- `music-director`

## Workflow

1. Identify the harmonic role and groove role of the bass part.
2. Favor root motion, strong guide tones, and rhythmic clarity over excess note density.
3. Check low-register spacing and interaction with kick or other bass instruments.
4. Differentiate sustained foundation from active melodic bass writing.
5. State assumptions about string count, tuning, or style when not provided.

## Guardrails

- do not overload low register with unnecessary chromatic noise
- do not treat bass as only root notes when the style needs motion
- keep line suggestions supportive of groove and arrangement space
