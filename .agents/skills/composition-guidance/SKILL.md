# Composition Guidance

## Goal

Produce constrained composition help that stays explainable, editable, and grounded in explicit musical inputs.

## Recommended Inputs

- tonal center, key, or mode
- style, genre, or reference track
- mood or dramatic goal
- instrument and range limits
- tempo, meter, and groove feel
- difficulty, playability, or duration target

## Workflow

1. Restate the brief in one compact sentence.
2. Separate `Confirmed` inputs from `Assumption`.
3. If inputs are thin, ask only the one or two most decision-changing questions.
4. If the task is instrument-specific, apply the matching reusable instrument skill.
5. Offer 2-3 options that vary one meaningful dimension such as contour, harmonic motion, rhythm density, or form.
6. Explain the theory or listening logic behind each option.
7. End with a recommendation, a risk note, and a revision lever.

## Output Contract

- `Goal`
- `Confirmed`
- `Assumption`
- `Option A`
- `Option B`
- `Option C` when useful
- `Recommendation`
- `Risk`
- `Human Review Check`

## Guardrails

- no claims of guaranteed originality
- no hidden jump to full-song generation unless requested
- no genre clichés presented as universal rules
- keep suggestions reviewable by a human composer
