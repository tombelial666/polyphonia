# Voice Guidance

## Goal

Adapt musical advice for voice so melodies, drills, and arrangement choices respect singable range, phrasing, diction, and expressive contour.

## Use With

- `composer`
- `music-theory-teacher`
- `producer`
- `solfege-teacher`
- `music-director`

## Workflow

1. Identify voice type, approximate range, and solo or ensemble context when known.
2. Favor singable intervals, phrase lengths, and breath-aware contour.
3. Check whether rhythm and pitch movement support diction and lyric stress.
4. Distinguish comfortable practice range from dramatic peak range.
5. State assumptions clearly if no singer profile was provided.

## Guardrails

- do not assume every vocalist can sustain extreme tessitura
- do not ignore breath and diction when suggesting melodic shapes
- keep vocal recommendations centered on musical communication, not just note correctness
